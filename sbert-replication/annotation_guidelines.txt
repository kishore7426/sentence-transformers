CONTROLLED SEMANTIC SIMILARITY DATASET — ANNOTATION GUIDELINES

Purpose
Rate the semantic similarity of each sentence pair independently of its category.

Scale
5 = Semantically equivalent: essentially the same meaning.
4 = Mostly equivalent: same central meaning with a small difference in detail.
3 = Strongly related but not equivalent: substantial shared meaning, but an important difference.
2 = Related topic with limited shared meaning.
1 = Slightly related: a weak topical or contextual connection.
0 = Unrelated or clearly incompatible meanings.

Instructions
1. Read both sentences completely before scoring.
2. Judge meaning, not just shared words.
3. Pay particular attention to negation, who did what to whom, numbers, locations, people, organisations, and other entities.
4. Do not change a score merely because wording is different if the meaning is preserved.
5. Record one integer from 0 to 5 in the human_score column.
6. Record an annotator identifier (for example A1 or A2) in the annotator column.
7. If uncertain between two scores, choose the score that best reflects the amount of meaning preserved and note the case separately for later review.
